# Placement evidence tables

These files transcribe the local research notes dated 13 November 2026, including `citation-audit-placement-tables.md` and `full-tables-from-five-papers.md`. Values retain their published units, columns, sample descriptions and source keys. The citation audit is itself a research note: a cell's `section` names it when that is where a value or correction was read. A source's `read` field records the fullest reading reported by the supplied notes and audit; it does not claim another reading of the original article. `secondary` means an opened guideline or a statement reported through another source. A full-text review can contain secondary statements, identified in its notes and the cell's qualifier.

The reader embeds the JSON with `include_str!`, parses it once and looks up exact coordinates. It never interpolates, converts units, averages sex columns, rounds to a size grid or substitutes a neighbouring cell. Absent cells mean no value. Counts, ratios, correlation coefficients, percentages, degrees and millimetres remain distinct. Approximation, comparison signs, anatomical conditions and interpretation limits remain in qualifiers.

## Reading a cell

Use `evidence::tables()` to obtain the immutable catalogue. `lookup(Table, &Coordinate)` returns `Option<&Cell>`; `cells(Table)` gives published rows in file order, and `source(key)` resolves their provenance. `Table::ALL` and `sources()` have stable iteration order. The files are parsed once, with no runtime file access. `lookup` excludes `Usage::PrintedOnly` observations and returns no value for their coordinates; `cells` retains them for inspection, so raw-cell callers must check `usage`. `Cell::whole_mm` marks a measurement printed to a whole millimetre; `decimals` preserves the central value's printed decimal count and `printed` keeps its literal text. All 72 Gao cells expose decimal precision, including all 36 short lengths and 19 widths printed to whole millimetres. The reader applies no precision adjustment.

A coordinate matches every field, including absent dimensions. Gao controls use key `outer-width`, group `gao-controls` and a level, without curve side, age band or sex. The historical `outer-width` key does not imply that Gao identifies outer cortical width: the paper leaves width type unspecified. Zheng width cells keep the published age band and sex separate. Upendra widths keep curve side separate. Adding a dimension that the published row does not have returns no value; absent dimensions are never wildcards.

The value enum preserves scalars, explicitly identified mean/SD pairs, unlabelled ± pairs, medians with optional printed IQR, ranges, enumerated sizes, named level ranges and named discs. The completed Upendra chords carry explicit SD, range and per-cell pedicle count. A remaining scalar observation has no implicit SD. Bare ± findings use `PlusMinus` unless a documented project interpretation assigns a statistic. Gao and Zheng never label their ± as SD; using it as SD is explicitly marked as this project's inference. Likewise, the reading of Zheng's first Table 1 row as inner and second as outer, negative angles as divergent, and Upendra convex as anatomical right are project inferences. These distinctions are recorded rather than attributed to the paper. There is no patient-frame geometry in these tables.

Coordinate migrations from the citation audit are exact: `first-cephalad-deviation-min` in group `deviated-vertebra` replaces `liv-plus-one-deviation-min` in `wang-2011`. In `selective-lumbar`, `tll-bending-cobb-max` replaces `thoracic-bending-cobb-max` to retain the printed TL/L wording. The separate `cobb-ratio-min` in `sanders-selective-lumbar` is 1.25 with an inclusive comparison. In each stop-short group, `studies` and `patients` retain their proximal-comparison meaning; new `distal-studies` and `distal-patients` identify the distal comparison. Old renamed coordinates return no value. Chiu's former all-pedicle wall coordinate is replaced by the physical-side groups and unresolved-side per-level rows described below.

No source is selected by the reader: callers choose a cohort and age band, combine columns or apply their own rule. A malformed bundled file is caught by tests; initialisation panics if it is nevertheless shipped. Unknown fields, unknown unit/read-status/value variants, explicit null table dimensions, repeated coordinates and unresolved or repeated source keys are refused.

## Citation schema

`sources.json` uses the same wrapper and list shape as the load-bench source registry: `{"sources": [ ... ]}`. Every record contains `key`, structured `citation` (`authors`, `year`, `title`, `journal`, `volume`, `issue`, `pages`), `pmid`, `doi`, `pmcid`, `read`, `n`, `population`, `label`, `url` and `notes`. Unsupported bibliographic fields are explicitly null. `n` retains descriptive sample information rather than manufacturing one pooled number. The original note sections and source qualifications are in `notes`. The Rust `Source` now holds a structured `Citation`, `key`, `pmcid`, `label` and `notes`; its `n` and `url` are optional strings. Existing `source(key)` and `sources()` signatures are unchanged. `PublishedValue::Median` adds an optional `iqr`, and `Unit::Correlation` identifies correlation coefficients. `Source` retains the same form through the complete-table pass.

The 51 identified publications have titles and at least one PMID or DOI, copied exactly from the supplied audit records. Kuraishi is labelled `conference-abstract`; the other identified papers are labelled `peer-reviewed`. The guideline is labelled `guideline`, and three mixed or ambiguously attributed note summaries are labelled `research-note-summary`. The 11 added papers received bibliographic confirmation only: their original read status is retained and their notes explicitly say “bibliographic record confirmed; numbers not audited”. Their bibliographic records do not establish a new numerical reading.

## Missing and pending information

No missing cell is stored as zero or null. Missing table dimensions are omitted; a lookup then returns no value. Null bibliographic fields mean the authorised audit record does not supply that field. Only `ao-lenke`, `screw-p1-hard-floor`, `screw-p4-summary` and `fusion-level-definitions` have both PMID and DOI null; these are not unidentified papers.

The formerly pending Zheng and Upendra transcription is present. No complete-table measurement cell is missing: Zheng has 360 observations (72 for each of five measurements), and Upendra has 102 primary observations (34 for each of three measurements), plus five conflicting Table 4 alternatives. Excluded cells remain present for inspection but return no usable value. Zheng's malformed `4.83 ± 072` is stored with literal text and only its unambiguous central scalar 4.83; no numerical spread is invented or repaired. The complete table takes precedence over the earlier rounded chord/angle samples. Printed prose ranges remain separate summaries, even where they fail to contain every table mean.

Still absent from these papers: Zheng's age-band/sex sample counts and lumbar measurements; Sato's per-cell counts; Chiu's per-region counts and resolved curve side for T7/T8; Kuraishi's per-level diameter/chord numbers; Balikci's per-level observed screw lengths. These are not filled from cohort totals or regional summaries.

The four Sia kyphosis IQRs are also pending. Other information still not supplied includes Gao lumbar widths, mirror aorta-warning levels for left thoracic curves, Sato regression coefficients, a neutral-rotation degree threshold or grade conversion, population body-width references, population adjustments, actual adolescent head height and rod overhang. The S1–LIV abstract does not define enough geometry to compute its angle. Mechanics Table A's rod grid and section properties are project inferences and are not introduced as paper-backed cells here.

The full-text Liu SV−1 OR remains 2.60, taking precedence over the abstract's 2.59 and the misquoting later review. Chen's PPV remains 55.5%, taking precedence over the synthesis's rounded 55%. Sato's 26.3% under the expansion condition and 71.9% under 4 mm outer width are distinct findings. No new numerical average, conversion, interpolation or rounding is made by this reader.
